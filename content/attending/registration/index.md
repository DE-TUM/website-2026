### Main Conference 
The Main Conference option includes:
- Access to all main conference sessions (12–14 May)
- Lunch and coffee breaks during the main conference (12–14 May)
- Posters & Demos session and reception on (12 May)
- Gala Dinner (13 May)
- Access to project networking session

### Pre-Conference 
The Pre-Conference option includes:
- Access to Workshops/Tutorials/Ph.D. Symposium sessions (10–11 May)
- Lunch and coffee breaks during both days (10–11 May)

### Additional Gala Dinner Ticket for an Accompanying Persons
Additional ticket for **Gala Dinner** (13 May): 90 EUR (VAT included).

### Attendance and Accommodations
Check our dedicated page on [travelling and accommodation options](/attending/venue).

### Invitation Letters
For presenters who need an invitation letter, please contact us at [eswc2026-reg@easychair.org](mailto:eswc2026-reg@easychair.org).

### On-site Registrations
During the conference (10-14 May), the registration desk will be open daily from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
