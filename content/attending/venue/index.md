## Conference Venue
We are delighted to welcome you to [Royal Princess Hotel Dubrovnik](https://www.hotelroyalprincess.com/en/), the official venue of ESWC 2026.

Located along the beautiful Lapad coastline, the hotel offers elegant seaside accommodation, modern conference facilities, and exceptional hospitality all within easy reach of Dubrovnik’s historic Old city.

## Alternative Accommodations
Early booking is highly recommended.
Please note that the high season in Dubrovnik — particularly in the Old Town — begins in mid-May. Many visitors choose to stay in the Lapad area as a more budget-friendly alternative.
