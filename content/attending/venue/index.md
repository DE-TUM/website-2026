## Traveling 
Main directions you can fly from/to Dubrovnik

### Within Croatia
* Zagreb (Croatia Airlines)

### Europe – examples of direct routes
* UK: London (Gatwick, Heathrow, Stansted), Birmingham, Manchester, Bristol, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Belfast, Leeds/Bradford, Cardiff,
* Germany: Berlin, Frankfurt, Munich, Hamburg, Cologne/Bonn, Düsseldorf, Memmingen, Karlsruhe/Baden-Baden
* Italy: Rome Fiumicino, Milan Bergamo, Bari, Naples
* France: Paris (CDG + Beauvais), Bordeaux, Lille, Lyon, Marseille, Nantes, Nice/Basel area
* Netherlands: Amsterdam, Rotterdam
* Nordics: Copenhagen, Helsinki, Oslo, Bergen, Stavanger, Stockholm, Gothenburg
* Other: Brussels, Zurich, Geneva, Vienna, Athens, Thessaloniki, Warsaw, Krakow, Wrocław, Dublin, Cork, Belgrade, Barcelona, Madrid, Luxembourg city, etc.
